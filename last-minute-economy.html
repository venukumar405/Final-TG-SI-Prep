National income terms

TermMeaning
GDPValue of all final goods and services produced within the country in a year.
GNPGDP + Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA).
NDP / NNPGDP − Depreciation / GNP − Depreciation.
National IncomeNNP at factor cost = NNP at market price − indirect taxes + subsidies.
GVA (basic prices)GDP − product taxes + product subsidies. Sector growth is measured in GVA.
Per capita incomeNational income ÷ population.
Nominal vs realNominal = current prices; real = constant (base-year) prices. GDP deflator = nominal ÷ real × 100.
MethodsProduction (value added), Income, Expenditure (C + I + G + X − M).
Who estimatesNational Statistics Office (NSO), MoSPI. Base year now 2022-23 (old: 2011-12).

Inflation

TermMeaning
Demand-pullToo much money chasing too few goods.
Cost-pushRise in input costs — wages, oil, raw material.
StagflationHigh inflation + low growth + unemployment.
Deflation / DisinflationFall in price level / fall in the rate of inflation.
ReflationDeliberate raising of prices to come out of deflation.
Headline vs coreHeadline = all items; core = excluding food and fuel.
CPIRetail prices — NSO, MoSPI (base 2024, released 12 Feb 2026). Used for inflation target.
WPIWholesale prices — Office of Economic Adviser, DPIIT (base 2022-23). No services.
Inflation target4% with 2–6% band, 1 Apr 2026 – 31 Mar 2031 (second renewal; first 2016).
