Privacy policy

SI & PC Prelims Prep · Last updated: 20 November 2026

SI & PC Prelims Prep ("the app") is a free practice app for candidates preparing for the Telangana police recruitment Preliminary Written Tests. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Summary

The app does not ask for, collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There is no sign-up, no account, no advertising and no analytics.

Information stored on your device

Your answers, scores, chosen exam date and practice progress are saved only on your own device, in the app's local browser storage. This data never leaves your device and the developer cannot see it. You can delete it at any time with About & privacy → Reset all progress, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Network requests

When you are online, the app downloads its own files from GitHub Pages (github.com) and its fonts from Google Fonts. As with any website, these services receive standard technical information such as your device's IP address and browser type to deliver the files. The app does not send any of your answers or progress with these requests. See the GitHub privacy statement and the Google privacy policy.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, files or any other device permission.

Children

The app is intended for adult recruitment candidates. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Not an official app

The app is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Telangana State Level Police Recruitment Board (TGPRB/TSLPRB), Telangana Police or the Government of Telangana. Official recruitment information is available only at https://www.tgprb.in.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/venukumar405/Final-TG-SI-Prep/issues or use the developer email shown on the app's Google Play listing.
