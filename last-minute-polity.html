Institutions and processes

Union and State executive, Parliament, judiciary, emergency, local government, constitutional vs statutory bodies, all major amendments, landmark judgments and commissions.

President (Arts 52–62)

PointDetail
ElectionIndirect — electoral college of elected MPs + elected MLAs of States, Delhi and Puducherry; proportional representation by single transferable vote (Art. 55)
Eligibility / term35 years; 5-year term; can be re-elected
Impeachment (Art. 61)'Violation of the Constitution'; either House; 14 days' notice; 2/3 of total membership of each House. Nominated members take part
PowersPardon (Art. 72); Ordinance (Art. 123); appoints PM, CJI, judges, CAG, CEC, Governors; Emergency (352, 356, 360)
VetoAbsolute, suspensive, pocket veto (no time limit — Zail Singh, Indian Post Office Bill 1986). No veto on Constitution Amendment Bills (24th Amendment)

Vice-President (Arts 63–71)

PointDetail
ElectionMembers of both Houses (elected + nominated); no MLAs
RoleEx-officio Chairman of Rajya Sabha; acts as President when office is vacant (max 6 months)
RemovalRajya Sabha resolution by effective majority, agreed by Lok Sabha; 14 days' notice; no formal impeachment

Prime Minister and Council of Ministers (Arts 74–78)

PointDetail
ResponsibilityCollectively responsible to Lok Sabha (Art. 75(3))
SizeNot more than 15% of Lok Sabha strength (91st Amendment, 2003)
Art. 78PM's duty to keep the President informed

Parliament (Arts 79–122)

PointDetail
Lok SabhaMax 550 (530 States + 20 UTs) under Art. 81; now 543. Term 5 years; age 25. Seat share frozen on 1971 Census
Rajya SabhaMax 250 (238 + 12 nominated); now 245. Permanent House — 1/3 retire every 2 years; age 30. Telangana: 7 seats
Money Bill (Art. 110)Only in Lok Sabha; Speaker certifies; Rajya Sabha must return within 14 days
Joint sitting (Art. 108)Speaker presides; not for Money or Constitution Amendment Bills. Held thrice: Dowry Prohibition 1961, Banking Service Commission (Repeal) 1978, POTA 2002
DevicesQuestion Hour (first hour); Zero Hour (Indian innovation, 1962); No-confidence motion (only Lok Sabha, 50 members to admit); Adjournment motion
Financial committeesPAC 22 (15 LS + 7 RS; chair from Opposition); Estimates 30 (all LS — largest); COPU 22
SessionsBudget, Monsoon, Winter; gap between sessions not more than 6 months; quorum 1/10

Judiciary

PointDetail
Supreme Court (Art. 124)Began 1950 with CJI + 7 judges; now CJI + 37 (SC Number of Judges Amendment Act 2026, in force 16 May 2026). Retire at 65
AppointmentCollegium system (Second Judges case 1993, Third 1998); NJAC struck down 2015
RemovalProved misbehaviour or incapacity — special majority in each House (Judges Inquiry Act 1968)
JurisdictionOriginal (Art. 131), writs (Art. 32), appellate (132–134), SLP (Art. 136), advisory (Art. 143), review (Art. 137), court of record (Art. 129); curative petition (Rupa Hurra 2002)
High Courts25 HCs; Art. 214; judges retire at 62; writs under Art. 226 (wider than Art. 32 — also for legal rights)

States

PointDetail

Rapid self-check — tap to see the answer

1. Who presides over a joint sitting of Parliament?

Speaker of Lok Sabha.

2. How many times has a joint sitting been held?

Three times — 1961, 1978, 2002.

3. Which states have Legislative Councils?

Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Bihar, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh.

4. Which emergency has never been declared?

Financial Emergency (Art. 360).

5. Which is the largest parliamentary committee?

Estimates Committee (30, all from Lok Sabha).

6. Present sanctioned strength of the Supreme Court?

CJI + 37 judges (Act of 2026).

7. Which body is statutory — NHRC or NCSC?

NHRC (statutory); NCSC is constitutional (Art. 338).

8. Which amendment brought NCBC under the Constitution?

102nd (2018).

9. When did the 73rd Amendment come into force?

24 April 1993.

10. Which case limited misuse of Article 356?

S.R. Bommai (1994).

11. Writs under Art. 226 are wider than Art. 32 because…?

HCs can issue writs for legal rights too, not only Fundamental Rights.

12. Which committee recommended Fundamental Duties?

Swaran Singh Committee (1976).
