EOS-05 satellite (GISAT-1A)

Science, space & environment
  • Launched 4 Sept 2026 by GSLV-F17 (19th GSLV flight) from the Second Launch Pad, Sriharikota.
  • India's first imaging (Earth observation) satellite in geosynchronous orbit — continuous 'eye in the sky' over India; also called GISAT-1A.
  • ≈2,367 kg — India's heaviest Earth-observation satellite; placed in a sub-geosynchronous transfer orbit.
  • ISRO's first successful mission of 2026 after the PSLV-C62 failure in January. ISRO Chairman: V. Narayanan.

★ EOS-05 = GISAT-1A on GSLV-F17

UPI changes 2026

Economy & schemes
  • From 15 Oct 2026: 0.4% MDR (Merchant Discount Rate) on eligible person-to-merchant (P2M) UPI payments above ₹2,000; capped at ₹300 for payments of ₹75,000+.
  • P2P transfers and P2M payments up to ₹2,000 stay free; merchants cannot pass the MDR to customers. Flat ₹5 for some categories (railways, telecom, insurance, fuel).
  • Record: 21.63 billion UPI transactions in Dec 2025. NPCI = National Payments Corporation of India (2008); UPI launched 2016.
  • RBI's Payments Regulatory Board (chaired by Governor Sanjay Malhotra) held its first meeting in 2026.

★ UPI: 0.4% above ₹2,000 (P2M only)

Vikram-1 rocket (Skyroot Aerospace)

  • India's first privately built orbital rocket, by Skyroot Aerospace, Hyderabad (CEO Pawan Kumar Chandana).
  • Maiden flight 'Mission Aagaman' — 18 July 2026, First Launch Pad, Sriharikota; reached ~450 km low Earth orbit (60° inclination).

  • Earlier: Vikram-S suborbital flight (Mission Prarambh), 18 Nov 2022. Skyroot — India's first space-tech unicorn. Next: Vikram-2 (1,000 kg), 2027.

★ Vikram-1 = Skyroot, Aagaman, 18 July
